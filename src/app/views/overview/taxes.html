Not tax advice. Figures are worked out from the history Cyrus could fetch from your connected exchanges, valued in USD at the time of each trade, with dates in UTC. Coins are tracked per exchange account; moving them between two of your connected exchanges keeps their original cost and date. USD and dollar stablecoins are treated as cash. Anything Cyrus can't account for (coins deposited from elsewhere, or sold beyond the history it has) is flagged as missing cost basis rather than guessed. Use Fix to fill those gaps from your own records, and review the export with your tax software or accountant before filing.
