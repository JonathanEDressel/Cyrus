## New: Charts

A full charting page, in the sidebar under Portfolio.

- **Crypto from Coinbase, Kraken or KuCoin.** Coinbase is the default and scrolls back through years of history. Kraken shows its most recent candles, and KuCoin covers the smaller coins. Every timeframe from 1 minute to 1 week.
- **US stocks and ETFs.** Add your own free Alpaca market-data key on **Profile → Exchanges** and pick **Stocks** in the symbol picker. Search by ticker or company name. Prices are real time from IEX; volume counts IEX trades only. Stocks are for charts and alerts: Cyrus doesn't trade them.
- **Drawing tools**: trend lines, horizontal levels, channels, Fibonacci retracements, measurements and more, with undo and redo.
- **Indicators**: moving averages, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD, VWAP, volume profile, OBV, money flow and others.
- **Pine Script editor**: write your own indicators, or paste one in.
- **Compare**: stack up to four symbols in their own panes, or overlay them in percent.
- **My trades**: your open orders, past fills and average cost drawn right on your crypto charts.
- **Price alerts** straight from a line on the chart. Cyrus checks them every minute, catching wicks between checks, and tells you with a desktop notification and, if email is set up, an email. You can also alert on your own Pine indicators while Cyrus is running.
- **Turn a line into an automation**: start a price rule from the chart.
- **Save your charts**, and put one on the Overview with the new **Chart** widget.

## Also

- The Privacy page lists Alpaca, which is only contacted if you add a key.
